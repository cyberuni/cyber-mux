Follow the AXI conventions of clibuilder 12.2: a bare `cyber-mux` now answers with a home view (`bin`, `description`, the multiplexer, the live panes or `panes: none`, the verbs, and next steps) instead of a usage error, and every command carries `examples` that show in its `--help`. Requires `clibuilder` `^12.2.0`.
